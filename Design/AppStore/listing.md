## Description
Sculpt3D is a precise 3D modeler built for touch. Sketch a profile, pull it into a solid, round its edges and keep going: every step is saved in an editable history, so you can change a dimension at any time and the whole model updates.

Under the hood is the same kind of boundary-representation geometry kernel used by professional CAD tools, so your parts are exact solids, not meshes. They export cleanly to the CAD app, 3D printer or laser cutter you already use.

SKETCH
• Lines, rectangles, circles, arcs, polygons and splines
• Constraints and dimensions that keep the sketch exactly where you want it
• Sketch on any plane or directly on a face of your model

MODEL
• Extrude, revolve and push-pull faces
• Union, subtract and intersect bodies
• Fillets, chamfers and shells
• Move, rotate, scale, mirror and pattern bodies
• Scatter objects across a surface and build terrain for dioramas

EDIT WITH CONFIDENCE
• A full parametric history: reopen any step and change it
• Variables and expressions drive your dimensions
• Unlimited undo and redo

INSPECT AND SHARE
• Measure distances, section views and multiple display modes
• Colours and materials for presentation renders
• Export STEP, IGES, BREP, STL, 3MF, OBJ, GLB, USDZ, DXF, SVG and PDF
• Import STEP, IGES, STL and BREP files

MADE FOR iPHONE AND iPAD
• Designed for multitouch and Apple Pencil, with keyboard and trackpad support on iPad
• Files live in the Files app and iCloud Drive like any other document
• Works fully offline. Sculpt3D collects no data.

## App Review notes
No account or sign-in is needed. Tap New Document, then use Insert to add a shape, or Sketch to draw a profile and extrude it. The History panel (clock icon) lists every step; tap one to edit it.
